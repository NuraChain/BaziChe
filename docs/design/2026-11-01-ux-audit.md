# The whole app's feedback, errors and layout: the audit

The owner's brief, 2026-11-01: "Full Application UX, UI Consistency, Interaction Feedback &
Reliability Improvement", an implementation pass over every page so that every interaction says it
was received, what is loading or pending, what succeeded or failed and why, and what to do next. This
file is its ledger: how the app was looked at (1), what was wrong and what put it right (2), what was
looked at and found right (3), and what is left with the part it belongs to (4). It is written as the
work lands; every number was measured on the production build unless it says otherwise.

## 1. How it was looked at

**The code.** Every place a request can fail and every place a failure is said: the `catch` blocks,
the `.catch` handlers, the readers of refusals (`whyRefused`, `whyShop`, `whyDonate`, `whyMarket`,
`whyParty`, `whyStaffRefused`), `attempt`, the toast queue and its promise form, and every
`ErrorState` panel.

**The browser.** Every route of the app, the public pages and the admin (45 in all) at 390, 768,
1280 and 1440 in English and Persian: 360 cells, the first screen of each, and the whole length at
390 and 1280. The verify copy's built server on a database seeded the way a development boot seeds
it, signed in as the `dana.w` fixture, with that wallet as the owner so every admin section drew.
Each cell measured sideways overflow, anything pressable or fillable with no accessible name, text
cut off by an ellipsis, and what the console said; the pictures were looked at where the numbers
pointed and at the widths where the side panel narrows the middle column. Failures were provoked by
hand in a real browser: the network taken away, the server stopped.

## 3. What was looked at and found right

- No route at any of the four widths, in either language, scrolls sideways (360 cells).
- Nothing pressable or fillable is without a name (360 cells).
- The console says nothing the product should not: NuraChain's 503s (no chain runs on the machine
  the audit ran on, so the profile, the NFTs and the public face say they could not read it, as they
  should) and the 404s two routes ask for on purpose (a check page for a made-up game, the page that
  is not there).
- No empty `catch`. 116 `.catch(() => undefined)`: each one a courtesy after something that already
  happened (a read again, a ring, a notice), and the user-facing ones say so where they are written.
- Persian at 390: the hero, the games row, the chats list and the bars read right to left with
  nothing cut.
- Under a slow line, every admin list already waited for its answer (`page !== null`) before it said
  it was empty, and so did the chat list, a thread, the watch list, the leaderboard, the gift
  history, the market positions and the vault's games; Home's live count arrives over the socket,
  which a slow api does not hold, so the number it shows early is a real one.
- Forms left as they were: the create form (its choices are chips, and the one way to be wrong, too
  many friends picked, is said beside them) and the owner's single-box calls in Money (one box beside
  one button, already marked and described).
- Every list that can outgrow a screen already pages: numbered pages from rail width and "Show more"
  on a phone, or a keyset's "Show older", each kept in the address. Every list long enough to be
  looked through already has its search and its filters (friends, a group's members, the chat list,
  the search page, the notifications, the watch list, the leaderboard, the games, the shop and every
  staff list), so none was added.
- No list asks for a sort the reader chooses. Each already stands in the order its reader looks for:
  newest first (the notifications, the staff's log, reports, orders, rewards, campaigns and people,
  the games being played and the tables waiting, a person's looks and history, gifts, positions),
  the reader's own place first and then rank (the leaderboards, the top inviters), online first (the
  friends and the pickers), pinned and then the latest line (the chat list), the catalogue's own
  order (the shop, Admin › Play and Shop), and fullest first (quick play). A second order would be a
  control with nothing to find by it.
- Left with no way back of their own, on purpose: the pickers in sheets (the create form's friends, a
  table's invitations, adding to a group), whose field's own clear stands right above; a person's
  looks, whose All chip is counted and right above; and a staff queue's status (reports, checks,
  rewards), each a list of its own whose emptiness is a fact, not a narrowing.

- Every other control with an icon and no words is an `IconButton`, whose tooltip is built in: a scan of
  every hand-written button and link whose only content is an icon found the one in F20. Left
  without a tooltip on purpose: a table row's time control ("10+0") sits inside the row's link, where
  a second thing to focus would break the link, and it says "Time control" to a screen reader.
- A state is the app's `Badge` wherever one is shown (orders, gifts, positions, reports, a look's
  status, a game's switch, a member of the staff's role). The pills drawn by hand that are left are
  marks, not states: a stake, a table of two sides, a clock, the reader's own row on a board, a count.
- All 64 empty states were read as one list. A queue or a fact says so and offers nothing (reports,
  checks and rewards waiting, nobody online, no requests, no notifications, no devices yet, no NFTs);
  "nothing yet" says what fills it and the way there; "nothing found" offers the way back (UX5); "it
  could not be read" offers Try again (UX3).

- The fallbacks the whole app shares, beside F21 to F24. No page says "forbidden", on purpose: a page
  a reader may not open answers as one that is not there, because a refusal is not an oracle, and an
  action the server refuses with a 403 says "You are not allowed to do that, so nothing changed."
  (UX2). A failure nothing nearer catches still meets the boundary over every route ("The lights went
  out.", Try again reloading once the server answers). A server that is down when the app starts
  is the held page, which asks again by itself and opens when it answers; one that goes down while a
  page is open is the connection strip in the app, and every read and action that fails says the
  server is not answering, and every store reads itself again once the socket is back.
- Navigation as a whole. Back goes where the reader came from, counted by the router's own
  distance; a list keeps its page, its filters and its search in the address, so a refresh and a
  shared link open it as it was; a page left while its read was out never draws the late answer
  (UX3, UX5); and an action that fails leaves the reader where they were, with what they typed (UX4).
  No breadcrumb was added: every page below a section has the top bar's way back to the page above
  it, and no page is more than two steps below its section.

- The keyboard, walked on the built server. A tooltip opens on a keyboard's focus and names its
  control (the sidebar's fold, at 1280 in Persian), and not on a focus the page moved itself. Alt+T
  takes the keyboard to the newest toast's own button and Escape closes it and gives the keyboard
  back to what it was on (the copy chip on `/app/me`). A sheet takes the keyboard to its first field
  (Add friend), Escape closes it and gives the keyboard back to its opener, and Tab goes round the
  sheet and the browser's own bar and never into the page, which is inert while a sheet stands: no
  trap is needed where the page behind cannot be reached.
- What the probe of every route measured (`ux8-a11y.mjs`: 390 and 1280, both languages, forty Tab
  stops a cell, reduced motion asked for). Out of a finger's reach: the two put right (F26, F28).
  Under 24px under a mouse: links on a 20px line, each alone in its row and so clear of any other
  target by the spacing WCAG 2.5.8 allows, and "How this works", which is 44px under a finger. Focus
  that could not be seen on the element itself: the search fields and the staff's text boxes, whose
  focus is drawn on the box round them (`focus-within`); measured on the friends search in both
  languages and an announcement's line in Persian, the box turns from the hairline to the accent.
  Under reduced motion nothing on the app's pages runs once settled; the public header's
  `header-settle` does, because its timeline is the reader's own scroll, and it fades its ground
  rather than moving anything.
- Nothing says a state by colour alone. A list on its way says "Loading…", once (F27, F30), and a
  spinner its label; a failure is a sentence (UX2, UX3); a wrong field is marked, described by its
  error and said under it (UX4); a status is a `Badge` whose dot carries the colour and whose word
  the state.
